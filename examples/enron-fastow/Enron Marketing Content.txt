ENRON: Integrity, Powered Forward
Brand copy draft — prepared by the agency team

At Enron, ethics isn’t a department. It’s the foundation every deal is built on.

Our four core values are Respect, Integrity, Communication and Excellence, and they aren’t just words carved into the lobby wall in Houston. They guide how 20,000 employees show up every day. We treat others as we’d want to be treated. We work with customers and prospects openly, honestly and sincerely. And when we say we’ll do something, we do it.

Enron didn’t become one of America’s most admired companies by cutting corners. We got here by reimagining what an energy company could be. We turned markets that were rigid and closed into ones that are open, efficient and fair, and that brought lower costs and more choice to families and businesses everywhere. Innovation and accountability go together, and our people take both seriously.

Our commitment goes beyond the bottom line. Across the communities we serve, Enron invests in schools, the arts and local causes, because a company is only as strong as the neighborhoods it calls home. Our financial reporting is reviewed by some of the most respected auditors in the business, so our shareholders, partners and employees can trust that what they see is what they get.

The future of energy is being written right now. At Enron, we’re writing it honestly.
