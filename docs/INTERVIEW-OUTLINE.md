# Witness interview outline

A template for planning a practice interview, based on the *Template Witness Interview Outline*. Fill in the [BRACKETED] parts for your case. It's a guide, not a script: the rubric rewards adapting to the witness's answers rather than reading straight through (see **Adaptability** in [ASSESSMENT.md](ASSESSMENT.md)).

The grader reads a summary of this outline as reference for what a well-run interview covers. It doesn't require the exact wording or order.

---

## [WITNESS NAME] outline

### Introduction of interviewers

### Upjohn warning

Give it at the very beginning, before any questions about the facts.

> [ENTITY] has retained [FIRM] to investigate [SUMMARIZE CONCERNS] that have been raised at the Company. The purpose of our interview today is to learn more about the facts surrounding those allegations so we can give legal advice to the Company.
>
> To be clear, in our roles here today, we represent the Company only. We do not represent you personally, and we are not your attorney, nor can we give you legal advice. Therefore, while this interview is confidential and subject to the attorney-client privilege, that privilege belongs to the Company, not to you personally, nor to us as the attorneys. This means that the Company gets to make the decision on whether to waive the privilege, meaning they may, at their discretion, choose to disclose any of the information we learn during the investigation. Thus, if the Company chooses to waive the privilege, it can do so without your consent and without telling you.
>
> That said, it is still important that you are fully open and truthful with us during the interview today. Similarly, to protect the privilege and the integrity of the investigation, it is very important that you not share the substance of our interview with anyone.
>
> Do you understand? Any questions?

**Explain the interview ground rules:** speculation, assumptions, estimates, and asking for clarification.

### Background

- Please state your current position.
- How long have you been in that position? With the organization?
- What positions have you previously held?
- Could you describe your current responsibilities? Have they changed over time? How?
- If tenure at the Company is less than 2 years: where did you work before, and why did you leave?
- Who do you report to? Does anyone report to you?
- Who do you work with most frequently? What departments or functions do you regularly interact with?
- Education?
- Do you maintain any specific credentials or licenses for your position?
- Do you maintain any other employment beyond the Company (e.g., consulting)?
- Do you own or manage any separate companies or LLCs?

### Background on processes

Before we turn to the specifics, we want to ask you about [PROCESS]:
- Can you describe how [PROCESS] generally works? Walk me through it from beginning to end.
- Who is ordinarily involved? Who initiates it? Who reviews or approves it? Who has final decision-making authority?
- Is that process documented anywhere? How are employees trained on it?
- Are there circumstances where the normal process changes? Who has authority to deviate from it? How are exceptions documented?
- Did the process work differently during [RELEVANT TIME PERIOD]? How? Who made that decision?
- What is your role in [PROCESS]?

### Questions about the specific issue

Now I want to turn to [EVENT / ISSUE]:
- Tell me everything you remember about what happened with [EVENT / ISSUE].
- What happened next? And after that? What else do you remember?
- Who else was involved? What conversations do you remember?
- Was there anything about what happened that seemed unusual to you at the time? What?

**Chronology of events**
- When did you first become aware of [ISSUE]? How did you first learn about it?
- Who else was present? What specifically were you told? What did you say?
- Did you communicate with anyone about it? Who? When?
- What happened next? What did you understand would happen? Did that happen?
- What was discussed afterwards? Was anything decided? Who made that decision?
- Were you involved in the decision? Did you agree with it?
- What happened after the decision? Was it communicated to anyone else?

### Decision-making and responsibility

- Who was responsible for [DECISION / ACTION]? What was your role? Did you approve of it?
- Were you consulted before the decision was made? Did you recommend a particular course of action? What?
- Who else was involved? Were alternatives discussed? What alternatives?
- Who ultimately made the decision? Was it documented? Where would that documentation be?

**If the witness attributes the decision to someone else**
- You said [PERSON / FUNCTION] handled that. What do you mean by that? What did they do?
- Did that person tell you that? If not, how do you know?
- Who might know more about that decision?

### Addressing inconsistencies

- Earlier, you told me [PRIOR STATEMENT]. Have I understood you correctly?
- You have now said [CURRENT STATEMENT]. Have I understood that correctly?
- Help me understand how those two statements fit together.
- Has your recollection changed? Did something refresh your memory?
- Which statement best reflects your present recollection? Why?
- Is there anything else about your earlier answer you would change or clarify?

### Documents

**Before showing the document**
- Do you remember communicating about [SUBJECT]? What do you remember? Who was involved? What was discussed?

**Showing the document:** I am now going to show you [DOCUMENT NAME / NUMBER].
- Do you recognize this document? What is it? Have you seen it before?
- When would you have received or reviewed it? What was its purpose?
- Please take a moment to review it.
- What does this document mean to you? What did you understand [NEUTRAL DOCUMENT LANGUAGE] to mean?
- Did you take any action after receiving it? Why? Did you discuss it with anyone?
- Earlier, you said [PRIOR ANSWER]. Is that still your recollection?
- This document states [ACCURATE, NEUTRAL CHARACTERIZATION]. Can you help me understand the difference?

### Wrap-up

- Is there anything you want to tell us, or that we didn't ask about, that you think we should know?
- Are there any documents that you believe would be helpful for us to see?
- Any other witnesses you think we should speak with?
- **Reminders:** confidentiality, record retention, and not to speak with other witnesses.
- If applicable, a reminder of the anti-retaliation policy.
