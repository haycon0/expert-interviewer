From: Enron Payroll Services
To: All Enron Houston Employees
Date: Friday, November 2, 2001, 9:14 AM
Subject: REMINDER: Direct deposit changes and year-end W-2 address updates

Good morning,

This is a reminder that changes to direct deposit accounts must be submitted by 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, November 7 to take effect for the November 15 pay date. Changes received after the deadline will take effect on the following pay date.

To make a change, log in to the eHRonline portal and choose Pay > Direct Deposit. You will need your bank's routing number and your account number. Please do not email account numbers to Payroll.

Year-end W-2 forms will be mailed to the home address on file as of December 14. If you have moved this year, please update your address in eHRonline by that date.

Paper paychecks for employees without direct deposit will continue to be available at the Payroll window, Enron Building, 3rd floor, from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. on pay dates.

Questions? Call the Payroll Help Line at extension 3-PAYS.

Thank you,
Enron Payroll Services
